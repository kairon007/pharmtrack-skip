PharmTrack is your personal medication and health management assistant. Designed to help you stay organized and informed about your healthcare journey.

- Store detailed consultation notes from doctor visits
- Manage and organize appointments

- No account creation needed

Perfect for managing chronic conditions, medication schedules, or simply staying organized with your healthcare needs.
